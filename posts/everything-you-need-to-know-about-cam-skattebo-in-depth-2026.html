His performance proved he was far too talented to remain outside the national spotlight.

1. Yards After Contact (YAC)

Skattebo routinely averages over 3.8 yards after contact per carry. He absorbs initial hits at the line of scrimmage, maintains balance through lower-body torque, and keeps moving forward. Defensive backs frequently bounce off his pads in open-field encounters.

2. Missed Tackles Forced (MTF)

According to PFF analytics, Skattebo maintains a Missed Tackle Forced rate exceeding 25% of his touches. His combination of effective stiff-arms, subtle lateral shifts, and violent hurdle attempts forces defenders into bad tackling angles.

3. Clutch Quarterback Efficiency

When lined up in Wildcat or executing trick plays, Skattebo holds a near-perfect passer rating on jump passes and rollouts. His decision-making in short-yardage scenarios adds a multidimensional layer to any playbook.

Scouting Report: 2026 NFL Draft Outlook

As NFL teams increasingly value positionless skill players who can contribute on all three downs, Skattebo fits the mold of a hybrid weapon.

Core Strengths

  • Elite Contact Balance: Sub-surface center of gravity enables him to absorb heavy hits and remain upright.
  • Versatile Skill Tree: Capable receiver out of the backfield, effective pass protector, and competent trick-play passer.
  • Relentless Motor: Plays with unyielding competitive intensity that elevates team culture.
  • Vision & Patience: Waits for zone blocks to develop before exploding through pressing lanes.

Areas for Growth

  • Top-End Breakaway Speed: Lacks elite 4.3-track speed to consistently outrun secondary defenders over 70+ yards.
  • Heavy Workload Wear: His physical style invites heavy collisions, requiring strict body maintenance and workload management.

Pro Player Comparisons

  • Stylistic Hybrid: A mix of Peyton Hillis (for sheer violence at the line), Austin Ekeler (for versatile receiving ability), and Mike Alstott (for goal-line reliability).

Who Should Draft Cam Skattebo?

  • Power-Run Offenses: Teams operating heavy 12-personnel and gap-scheme running systems will maximize his downhill power.
  • Creative Play-Callers: Offensive coordinators who love using non-traditional formations, Wildcat looks, and dynamic pass-catching backs.
  • Special Teams-Focused Squads: Teams looking for instant core contributions on coverage and punt units.
