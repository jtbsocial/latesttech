Using optical tracking camera systems installed across NFL stadiums, analysts measure separation created against single press-man coverage. Higgins wins against press coverage at an 84th percentile rate league-wide. His strength at the line of scrimmage allows him to defeat physical cornerbacks instantly.

2. Expected Points Added (EPA) per Target (+0.38)

When quarterbacks target Higgins in third-and-long scenarios, the team’s Expected Points Added increases by +0.38 points per dropback. This places him in elite company alongside receivers who function as primary safety blankets for elite quarterbacks.
