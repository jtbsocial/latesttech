How Houston's 17 Opponents Are Selected

  1. Six Divisional Games (Home and Away): Double-headers against AFC South rivals—the Indianapolis Colts, Jacksonville Jaguars, and Tennessee Titans.
  2. Four Intra-Conference Games: A complete rotation against one full AFC division (rotating every three years).
  3. Four Inter-Conference Games: A complete rotation against one full NFC division (rotating every four years).
  4. Two Position-Based Intra-Conference Games: Matches against AFC teams that finished in the exact same divisional standing position from the previous season.
  5. One 17th Cross-Conference Matchup: An extra inter-conference opponent based on prior season divisional standings.
