3. The Mechanics of His Game: Forehand, Backhand & Serve Analytics

The Forehand Drive

Kecmanovic’s forehand is his primary dynamic weapon. Utilizing a semi-western grip, he achieves a clean kinetic transfer from his lower body through the core, generating rapid racquet head speed. The ball leaves his racket with a heavy top-spin trajectory that jumps high off hard and clay court surfaces, forcing opponents behind the baseline.

The Two-Handed Backhand

Built on compact preparation and precise footwork, Kecmanovic's backhand is arguably his most reliable stroke under pressure. He excels at changing direction down the line, taking the ball early on the rise to eliminate opponent reaction time. This stroke serves as both a defensive shield against heavy cross-court forehands and an offensive springboards in rapid rallies.

Serve & Return Dynamics

While not reliant on height-driven serve bombs, Kecmanovic optimizes slice and kick serves to set up aggressive first-strike groundstrokes. His return of serve ranks among the most statistically sound on tour, maintaining a compact backswing that neutralizes 130+ mph serves with ease.
