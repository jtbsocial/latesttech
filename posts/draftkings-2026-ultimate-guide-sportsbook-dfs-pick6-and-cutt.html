The DraftKings Product Suite in 2026

DraftKings operates a multi-vertical ecosystem tailored to diverse player preferences. Understanding how these platforms interact helps maximize your play across the network.

1. DraftKings Sportsbook

The centerpiece of the ecosystem, DraftKings Sportsbook, sets industry standards for market depth. Users can access thousands of pre-match and in-play markets across professional leagues (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, Premier League, UFC) and niche international sports (table tennis, cricket, rugby, esports).

Key features include:
* Progressive Same Game Parlays (SGP+): Combine multiple SGPs from different games into a single, high-yield wager.
* Early Cash Out Engine: Dynamic cash-out values recalculated in milliseconds using live game data feeds.
* Odds Boosts & Profit Boost Tokens: Daily personalized profit multipliers delivered directly to user reward hubs.

2. Daily Fantasy Sports (DFS)

As the foundation of DraftKings, DFS remains a powerhouse in 2026. Classic salary-cap drafts, Showdown Slates, Tiered contests, and Guaranteed Prize Pools (GPPs) draw millions of entries weekly. Modern algorithm protections ensure casual users are separated from high-volume automated scripts in designated beginner and casual lobbies.

3. DraftKings Pick6

Pick6 is DraftKings' answer to peer-to-peer player prop fantasy games. Users build lineups of 2 to 6 player stat projections (e.g., More or Less than 255.5 Passing Yards). Unlike house-backed prop apps, Pick6 operates as a peer-to-peer contest where payouts scale based on correct selections and total pool liquidity, ensuring high compliance across various state jurisdictions.

4. DraftKings Casino

Available in states with legalized iGaming, DraftKings Casino features exclusive branded table games, live dealer studios streaming in 4K, and thousands of slot titles. In 2026, social casino elements allow friends to join private blackjack tables and share dynamic jackpot pools.

Micro-betting (betting on pitch-by-pitch or play-by-play outcomes) is fully integrated into the main Sportsbook interface. Users can place wagers on:
* Will the next pitch be a Strike, Ball, or In-Play Hit?
