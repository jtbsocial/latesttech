The ITIA Ruling & Career Interruption

In May 2024, the International Tennis Integrity Agency (ITIA) announced a provisional suspension for Bartůňková after testing positive for trimetazidine during in-competition testing at ITF events in February and March 2024.

  • Resolution: Following rigorous lab testing of unopened supplement batches, evidence substantiated that contamination was the primary vector. This resulted in a substantially mitigated suspension duration rather than a maximum multi-year ban.

This challenging period served as a trial by fire, reshaping her off-court mental endurance and training routines.
