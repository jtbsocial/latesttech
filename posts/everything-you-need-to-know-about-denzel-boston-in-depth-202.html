6. Scouting Report: Strengths & Areas for Growth

Key Strengths

  1. Elite Contested-Catch Ability: Dominates defensive backs at the catch point using superior hand-eye coordination and dynamic elevation.
  2. Physical Press-Release: Uses active hands and length to defeat line-of-scrimmage jams, preventing cornerbacks from disrupting route timing.
  3. Expansive Target Zone: Exceptional flexibility allows him to pull down off-target passes high, low, or behind his frame.
  4. Red-Zone Weaponry: A natural target in tight end-zone quarters, mastering back-shoulder throws and back-pylon fades.
