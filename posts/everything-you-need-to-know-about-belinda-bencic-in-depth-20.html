Early Success and WTA Breakthrough

  • Junior Dominance (2013): Bencic captured both the French Open and Wimbledon junior singles titles, catapulting her to the World No. 1 junior ranking.
  • Breakthrough on Tour (2014–2015): At just 17 years old, she reached the quarterfinals of the 2014 US Open. In 2015, she announced her arrival at the absolute top by winning the Rogers Cup in Toronto, defeating four top-10 players—including Serena Williams in a legendary semifinal encounter.
  • Injury Setbacks and Resilience: Injuries sidelined Bencic during her early twenties, requiring wrist surgery in 2017. However, her technical foundation enabled a triumphant comeback, winning Dubai in 2019 and reaching her first Grand Slam semifinal at the US Open later that year.

The Tokyo Olympic Glory (2021)

Bencic's career crowned its defining chapter during the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games. Playing with supreme confidence, she captured the Gold Medal in Women's Singles by defeating Marketa Vondrousova in a thrilling three-set final. She backed up this achievement by claiming the Silver Medal in Women's Doubles alongside Viktorija Golubic, etching her name alongside Swiss tennis royalty like Roger Federer and Martina Hingis.
