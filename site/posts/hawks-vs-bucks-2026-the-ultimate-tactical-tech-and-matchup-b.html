3. Tactical Schemes: How the Game Will Be Won

Atlanta's Game Plan: Five-Out Spread & High P&R Blitz

  1. Exploit the Drop Coverage: The Hawks utilize deep off-ball screens to force Milwaukee's big men into uncomfortable perimeter isolations.
  2. Early-Clock Transition: With a pace rating exceeding 102.0 possessions per 48 minutes, Atlanta aims to push the ball before Milwaukee's half-court defense can set up.
  3. Corner Dagger Generation: By collapsing the defense with mid-paint penetration, Atlanta ranks top-3 in wide-open corner three-point field goal attempts.

Milwaukee's Game Plan: Paint Neutralization & Board Control

  1. Funneling to the Help: Milwaukee builds a wall along the free-throw line, forcing Atlanta ball-handlers into mid-range pull-ups rather than high-value floater/rim attempts.
  2. Offensive Glass Domination: The Bucks leverage their physical size advantage to secure offensive rebounding percentages above 28.5%, generating critical second-chance points.
  3. Physical Perimeter Contests: Milwaukee wings use heavy physical contact on off-ball cutters to disrupt Atlanta's set rhythm plays.

5. Pros, Cons & Strategic Advantage

Atlanta Hawks Advantage Breakdown

  • Pros:

  • Unmatched fast-break points off defensive rebounds.
