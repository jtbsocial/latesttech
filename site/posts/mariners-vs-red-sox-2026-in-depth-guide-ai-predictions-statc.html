7. Strategic X-Factors: Bullpen Leverage & Park Factors

Bullpen High-Leverage Efficiency

In tight 2026 matchups, bullpen management relies on modern leverage index metrics (pLI). Seattle’s bullpen features high-velocity arm angles designed to maximize vertical approach angles (VAA). Boston’s bullpen counters with high pitch-tunneling efficiency, making their late-inning relievers exceptionally difficult to lay off in 2-strike counts.

Park Factor Dynamics

  • T-Mobile Park: Continues to favor high-spin, high-velocity pitchers. Run scoring is suppressed by approximately 5% relative to league baseline due to cold marine air during night games.
  • Fenway Park: Favors right-handed pull hitters and high line-drive hit profiles. The unique geometry increases doubles and triples probability while suppressing pure home run distance over right-center field.
