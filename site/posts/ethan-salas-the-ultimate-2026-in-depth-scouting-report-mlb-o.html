What set Salas apart was not merely raw physical toolset, but an unprecedented mental maturity. Defensive Mastery: Framing, Blocking, and Pop Time Tech

Pop Time Breakdown

Pop time measures the duration from the instant the ball hits the catcher's mitt to the precise moment it reaches the fielder's glove at second base. The MLB average pop time is approximately 2.01 seconds.

  • Ethan Salas Average Pop Time: 1.85 - 1.89 seconds
  • Exchange Rate: 0.62 seconds (elite hand-to-glove transfer)
  • Arm Velocity: 87.3 mph on direct throws to second base
