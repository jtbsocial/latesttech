A. High-Post Playmaking & Passing Vision

Avila operates as an offensive quarterback. When positioned at the top of the key or elbow, his spatial processing rate matches top-tier professional point guards. He manipulates weak-side help defenders with subtle head fakes and eyes, delivering precise skip-passes to corner shooters before the defensive rotation can close out.

  • Assist Rate (AST%): Ranks in the top 95th percentile nationally among all college center prospects.
  • Dribble Hand-Off (DHO) Efficiency: SLU runs a high volume of European-style 5-out Motion offenses, where Avila’s DHO reads generate over 1.18 points per possession (PPP).

B. Perimeter Stretch & Pick-and-Pop Mechanics

Unlike traditional interior players who clog the paint, Avila forces opposing rim protectors out to 25 feet. His release point, adjusted for clear vision through his trademark optical equipment, is consistent and quick for a player of his frame.

  • Pick-and-Pop Lethality: Defenses cannot drop beneath screens against Avila without surrendering uncontested, high-percentage three-pointers.
  • Pump-Fake and Drive Dynamics: When defenders close out aggressively, Avila utilizes precise pump-fakes followed by low-center-of-gravity drives into short mid-range floaters.

C. Post Footwork & Spatial Positioning

Inside the paint, Avila uses physics and low leverage to outmaneuver longer defenders. His drop-steps, up-and-under moves, and patient pivots allow him to score efficiently around the rim without requiring a 40-inch vertical jump.
