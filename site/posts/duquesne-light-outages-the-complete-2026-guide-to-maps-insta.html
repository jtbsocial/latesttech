Whether a sudden severe thunderstorm rips through the Ohio River Valley or heavy winter ice weighs down overhead distribution lines, dealing with an electrical outage requires fast access to actionable information.

Duquesne Light Company (DLC), serving more than 600,000 direct customers in Southwestern Pennsylvania, has undertaken massive grid modernization initiatives. In 2026, the utility relies heavily on AI-driven predictive maintenance, Automated Fault Location, Isolation, and Service Restoration (FLISR) systems, and advanced smart meters to track, isolate, and repair power disruptions faster than ever before.

This comprehensive 2026 guide covers everything you need to know about Duquesne Light outages: how to report them in seconds, how to read the live outage map like an expert, the exact sequence DLC uses to restore power, and how smart grid technology is changing local energy reliability.

How to Report a Duquesne Light Outage (Step-by-Step)

If your lights go out, do not assume your neighbors have already reported it. While DLC’s advanced smart meters automatically ping central dispatch servers during grid failures, reporting your specific address directly confirms localized transformer or service drop issues.

1. Duquesne Light Mobile App (iOS & Android)

The fastest interactive method in 2026 is the official Duquesne Light app.
* Log in using your registered account credentials or biometrics.
* Tap the red "Report Outage" icon on the main dashboard.
* Verify your service address and answer a two-second prompt regarding visible hazards (e.g., downed wires or sparking transformers).
* Enable push notifications to receive automated status updates as repair crews are dispatched.

2. SMS Automated Text Service

For users who prefer zero app clutter or have low bandwidth during storm surges:
* Text REG (Register) to 412-393-7000 using the cell phone number linked to your DLC account.
* Once registered, simply text OUT to 412-393-7000 whenever power fails.
* Text STAT at any point to receive the latest Estimated Time of Restoration (ETR) for your location.

3. Online Web Portal

  • Visit DuquesneLight.com/outages from any mobile browser or laptop.
  • Enter your 10-digit account number, primary telephone number, or physical street address.
  • Click Submit Report.

4. 24/7 Emergency Telephone Line

If you lack internet access or need to report an immediate life-threatening danger (such as downed power lines):
* Call 412-393-7000 directly.
* Follow the automated voice prompts to report an outage or speak to an emergency representative.
* Note: Always press the prompt for emergency hazards if you spot active flames or downed lines.
