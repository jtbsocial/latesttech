Arkansas enters 2026 with a offensive scheme centered around pre-snap motion and fast-paced tempo. By utilizing field-width spacing, Arkansas forces opposing defenses to make split-second communication decisions.

* Technology Factor: Arkansas coaches use helmet-communicator data and real-time press box analytics to track defensive personnel fatigue and call tempo spikes when mismatches are detected.
