In the rapidly evolving landscape of generative artificial intelligence, few companies have created as seismic a shift in such a brief window as Moonshot AI (月之暗面). Founded in early 2023 by Tsinghua University alumnus and AI visionary Yang Zhilin, Moonshot AI set out with a singular, disruptive thesis: long-context comprehension without information decay is the true gateway to Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).

When Moonshot AI first publicly rolled out Kimi (Kimi Chat), the platform immediately set social media and developer communities ablaze. While standard models struggled with context degradation beyond a few thousand tokens, Kimi debuted with a seamless 200,000-token context capability. Fast forward to 2026, and Kimi has evolved into a global powerhouse capable of ingesting whole code libraries, financial histories spanning decades, and entire academic disciplines in a single prompt.

Whether you are a software developer analyzing legacy code, a legal analyst auditing thousand-page contracts, or an academic researcher synthesizing hundreds of papers, Kimi has positioned itself as an indispensable productivity power tool.

1. Lossless Long-Context Attention Mechanisms

However, these shortcuts frequently led to the infamous "lost-in-the-middle" phenomenon, where models forgot facts buried midway through large texts.
